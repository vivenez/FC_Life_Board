About Us

Friendship Church is an evangelical, Bible-believing Christian church based in Izumo City, Shimane Prefecture.

The meetings are in English, but there are Japanese, Brazilian, Chinese and other friends who help with translations in their respective languages.

There is also a special class for children and young adults.

Please join us if you are in the area.
