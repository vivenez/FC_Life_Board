A Call to Return to the Lord

DATE: 2026-07-19

MAIN MESSAGE

On July 4th this year, America celebrated its 250th anniversary.

Whether you are American or from another nation, this milestone invites
us to reflect on God’s blessings and the urgent call He gives His
people.

I believe God has richly blessed America through its remarkable history.

America has demonstrated resilience and has enjoyed freedoms because of
the strong spiritual foundation laid by its founding fathers.

The men who risked their very fortunes and lives to build the foundation
of America understood that true greatness comes only from dependence on
Almighty God.

Today, the Lord speaks the same timeless truth to Friendship Church
believers from America, Japan, Brazil, and Colombia.

Notice this promise given to Solomon at the Temple’s dedication: “Then
if my people who are called by my name will humble themselves and pray
and seek my face and turn from their wicked ways, I will hear from
heaven and will forgive their sins and restore their land.” (2
Chronicles 7:14)

All Scripture is not written *to* us but it is written *for* us.

These words were written to Israel three thousand years ago, but still
speak with fresh urgency to God’s people at Friendship Church today.

Who are God’s people?

The Apostle Paul describes them clearly: For the grace of God that
bringeth salvation hath appeared to all (God's people), ¹² Teaching us
that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly,
righteously, and godly, in this present world;

¹³ Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the
great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ; (Titus 2:11-13)

God's people live spiritual lives as we look for Christ's return.

But what are God’s requirements for His children?

God's first requirement for His people is Humility.

God hates pride and says 'Pride goes before destruction and a haughty
spirit before destruction.' (Proverbs 16:18)

Every culture celebrates self-sufficiency, but Scripture calls believers
to recognize our complete dependence on God.

George Washington, America's first president, declared it impossible to
rightly govern without God and the Bible.

Washington was a good president who was known for being often on his
knees praying.

National or personal pride without humility before the Lord leads to
downfall.

We must lay aside arrogance, acknowledge our sin, and bow before the
King of kings.

When we stop relying on our own expertise and humbly cry out to God, He
moves powerfully.

God's second requirement is fervent, persistent prayer, not simply
casual words.

The early church prayed and saw God move (Acts 4).

When there is moral confusion as right is called wrong and wrong is
called right, the cure isn't political but in God’s people crying out.

Will you diligently pray for revival because the answer isn't in
Washington or Tokyo or Brasilia or Bogota - it's in prayer!

God's third requirement is devotion.

When God says, “Seek My face,” I believe He wants us to desire an
intimate relationship with Him, not just for His blessings or His help
in times of trouble.

In our distracted age where we're constantly looking and listening to
electronics of all shapes and sizes, we must return to the quiet altar
of devotion to God.

Seek God in His Word, through worship, and in daily communion.

Like a small child grabbing her father’s face to gain his full
attention, God calls us to truly focus on Him.

Finally, God calls for repentance as people “Turn from their wicked
ways.”

Repentance is a decisive turn away from idolatry, immorality, injustice,
and greed.

Forcefully reject the gender confusion being promoted today, especially
to our children, and reject everything that dishonors God.

It doesn't matter what sin you've committed, the good news remains that
"if we confess our sins to God, He is faithful and just to forgive us
our sins and to cleanse us from all wickedness.” (1 John 1:9)

You may be able to fool the people around you in this room, me included,
but you'll never fool God, so if there are secret areas where you need
to repent and turn fully to the Lord, you should do that today.

Whenever people or a nation fulfills God's requirements, then God
promises results.

God responds that 'He will hear from heaven.'

God attentively hears the cries of humble hearts and He acts by
forgiving their sin.

Through Jesus’ blood, forgiveness and cleansing are full and free.

And after repentance and forgiveness, God will heal their land.

“Godliness makes a nation great, but sin is a disgrace to any people.”
(Proverbs 14:34)

“Upright citizens are good for a city and make it prosper, but the talk
of the wicked tears it apart.” (Proverbs 11:11)

America's founders, like Patrick Henry, regarded Christianity as true
and spiritually valuable as they built an incredibly solid spiritual
foundation for the US that was based on the Bible.

Andrew Jackson, America's 7th president, called the Bible the rock of
the Republic.

Today in America, sometimes you'll hear: “Make America Great Again” but
Scripture is clear: greatness without godliness is impossible.

National righteousness flows from individual righteousness.

Every believer at Friendship Church, and across Japan, and all around
the world, must be spiritual as God commanded Solomon and Israel.

REFLECTION

The fate of any nation rests with God’s people.

The question is not whether any nation deserves God's blessing, but
whether believers in that nation will humble themselves, pray, seek
God's face, and repent.

I'd love to see what God could do if every Christian obeyed 2 Chronicles
7:14!

God can heal divisions, restore families, and revive churches.

God exalts nations - not because the people are perfect, but because He,
as almighty God, is merciful.

Any birthday, anniversary, or special event can be a time of reflection
and can become a turning point.

May we return to the Lord with all our heart and humble ourselves.

Let us pray fervently and seek God's face as we continually turn from
sin.

Then may Almighty God hear from heaven, forgive us and heal our lives,
heal our churches, and heal the nations around the world.

To God be all the glory - please join me in prayer.

# PRAYER

Our Heavenly Father, thank You for Your grace and mercy on America and
on every nation represented at Friendship Church today.

We humble ourselves before You and pray for purity and blessing in our
lives.

We ask for your blessing on Japan, Columbia, Brazil and America in
particular.

Help our governmental leaders to make spiritual decisions even when they
are not spiritual people.

We seek Your face and long for Your presence.

Cleanse us as we turn from our wicked ways.

Please hear from heaven, forgive our sins, and heal our world.

May spiritual revival come to all Your people.

In Jesus’ name we pray with thanksgiving,

Amen.
